ABSTRACT

CampusVenture is a production-deployed, multi-tenant software-as-a-service (SaaS) web platform engineered to bridge the critical structural divide between university student innovators, accredited angel investors, and collegiate incubation centres. While collegiate engineering campuses foster high concentrations of early-stage intellectual property, commercialization is frequently hindered by fragmented outreach, unverified pitch materials, lack of standardized investment diligence, and vulnerability to confidential document leakage.

The application unites three independent stakeholder dashboards into a single cryptographically secure ecosystem: (1) Student Founders build structured venture profiles, upload confidential pitch decks, manage a virtual data room, and receive 9-category AI-assisted investment readiness scoring; (2) Accredited Investors discover university-verified deal flow, filter opportunities by thesis and stage, request data room diligence access, execute legally binding mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), and review approved files; and (3) University Incubation Cells evaluate student venture submissions, verify student bona fides, and maintain a tamper-resistant real-time diligence audit ledger that synchronizes investor document interactions across institutions without cross-tenant data exposure.

CampusVenture is architected using React 19, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS on the presentation tier, powered by Supabase managed PostgreSQL, Supabase Auth, private Supabase Storage buckets, and deployed globally across Vercel Edge networks. Security boundaries are strictly enforced at the database level utilizing comprehensive PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies, domain-level institutional email filters (e.g. @sguk.ac.in), short-lived cryptographically signed document URLs, and resilient blob downloading mechanisms. The platform was validated on real campus ventures, including AutoCampus AI from Sanjay Ghodawat University, verifying complete end-to-end multi-tenant isolation, real-time audit logging, and responsive cross-device usability.

1. Introduction

In contemporary higher education institutions, undergraduate engineering and technology campuses have evolved into fertile grounds for student-led innovation, intellectual property incubation, and high-impact technology ventures. However, despite the surge in student ideation, an acute structural divide persists between collegiate innovators, institutional incubation authorities, and external angel investor networks. Student founders frequently encounter severe friction when attempting to communicate their technical progress, validate institutional affiliation, and secure pre-seed or seed capital from accredited early-stage investors.

Historically, campus-based entrepreneurship relies upon ad-hoc mechanisms: pitch presentations circulated via unencrypted email attachments, informal pitch sessions with fragmented investor notes, and manual paper-based Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs). This loose methodology exposes collegiate ventures to severe risks, notably the leakage of confidential intellectual property, unauthorized dissemination of sensitive financial cap tables, and complete absence of an auditable trail of diligence access. Simultaneously, angel investors face elevated transaction search costs and information asymmetry, as unverified student proposals lack standardized readiness metrics, verified academic credentials, and institutional vetting.

To address these structural inefficiencies, this field project conceives, architects, and implements CampusVenture—an enterprise-grade, multi-tenant software-as-a-service (SaaS) web platform specifically engineered for collegiate startup ecosystems. CampusVenture unifies three distinct user roles into an integrated, cryptographically isolated ecosystem:

  • Student Founders: Empowered to publish structured venture profiles, upload pitch materials, maintain an encrypted Virtual Data Room (VDR), and track real-time investor engagement and multi-category readiness scores.
  • Accredited Angel Investors: Provided with an institutional deal-flow exploration portal, granular sector and university filtering, automated mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement execution, and temporary signed-URL access to diligence assets.
  • University Incubation Centres: Equipped with an institutional governance cockpit to verify student bona fides, audit startup viability, review diligence activity, and maintain a tamper-resistant real-time audit ledger across institutional cohorts.

Developed using React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Supabase PostgreSQL, and Vercel Edge networks, CampusVenture enforces strict zero-trust multi-tenancy via database Row Level Security (RLS) policies and institutional domain verification, delivering a secure bridge from campus lab ideation to venture capitalization.

2. Literature Review
2.1 Background Study

The acceleration of student entrepreneurship and academic technology transfer has been widely investigated across software engineering and information systems literature. Early-stage venture financing models highlight that information asymmetry between founders and angel syndicates represents the single highest determinant of transaction failure. Traditional commercial platforms such as AngelList, Gust, and Crunchbase cater predominantly to established, incorporated legal entities possessing audited financial statements, historical revenue traction, and experienced executive teams. Consequently, nascent student enterprises emerging from academic laboratories are systematically marginalized within these mainstream platforms.

Specialized document-sharing utilities like DocSend and Dropbox Transfer provide page-level view telemetry; however, they operate as isolated document silos detached from institutional accreditation, multi-tenant academic governance, or legally integrated mutual NDAs. Conversely, university-managed learning portals (e.g. Moodle, Canvas) and generic Google Drive shared folders lack domain-specific venture evaluation rubrics, investor discovery pipelines, and cryptographic access enforcement.

Key Comparative Dimensions in Existing Incubation Systems:
  • Commercial Venture Marketplaces: Focus on late-stage seed/Series A; lack collegiate email validation, university endorsement badging, and multi-tenant university cohort filtering.
  • Generic Cloud Storage Services: Provide basic file hosting but lack automated bilateral NDA execution workflows, role-based diligence gates, and immutable audit logs for institutional compliance.
  • Institutional Academic Repositories: Focus on academic grading and thesis submission; completely divorced from external angel investors, capital tracking, and market-standard startup metrics.
  • Manual Incubation Cell Spreadsheets: Suffer from synchronization latency, manual email chains, human error in cap-table record keeping, and lack of mobile touch accessibility.

Recent research in multi-tenant SaaS security emphasizes that application-tier access control is vulnerable to privilege escalation and horizontal boundary crossing if not backed by database-level policy guarantees. The modern paradigm advocates for declarative database Row Level Security (RLS) combined with ephemeral cryptographically signed object retrieval, which guarantees that authorization policies are universally evaluated regardless of access pathway.

3. Motivation, Problem Statement and Objectives
3.1 Motivation

The primary motivation behind this field project stems from direct observation of high-potential student ventures at the Sanjay Ghodawat University Incubation Centre whose growth stagnated due to barriers in accessing external angel syndicates. While technical talent and inventive spirit thrive in collegiate departments, student founders often lack the legal frameworks and digital tools required to present institutional-grade diligence materials. Developing an automated, tamper-resistant incubation SaaS creates an accessible launchpad for young engineers to transform academic research into scalable enterprises.

3.2 Problem Statement

"To design, develop, secure, and deploy a responsive multi-tenant web platform that enables university student founders to curate verified venture profiles, safeguard intellectual property via digital mutual NDAs and virtual data rooms, undergo objective investment readiness scoring, and allow accredited angel investors and collegiate incubation cells to conduct streamlined diligence with complete row-level database isolation."

3.3 Objectives
  1. Multi-Tenant Architecture: Implement a robust multi-tenant architecture supporting three authenticated roles (Student Founder, Angel Investor, University Incubation Head) with isolated data perimeters.
  2. Institutional Domain Verification: Enforce strict identity onboarding using institutional email domain classifications (e.g. @sguk.ac.in) to eliminate unverified actor profiles.
  3. Virtual Data Room (VDR): Construct a private, encrypted document repository supporting multi-category file management (Pitch Decks, Cap Tables, Financial Models, IP Filings).
  4. Automated Mutual NDA Protocol: Embed an in-browser bilateral digital signature workflow that legally executes and cryptographically binds NDAs before granting access to confidential files.
  5. Ephemeral Signed URLs & Download Engine: Integrate short-lived tokenized storage URLs (60-second TTL) and cross-device blob download handlers to eliminate direct public bucket exposure.
  6. Quantitative Readiness Scoring: Formulate and compute an automated 9-category Investment Readiness Score (IRS) and Berkus pre-money valuation engine to eliminate subjective assessment bias.
  7. Realtime Diligence Audit Ledger: Architect an immutable, real-time audit logging engine that tracks document views, access grants, and signature events across university dashboards.
  8. Responsive Cross-Device Presentation: Build an adaptive, zero-overflow UI using React 19, Tailwind CSS, and safe-area touch components supporting screens from mobile to 4K displays.
  9. Zero-Trust Database Security: Author comprehensive PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies guaranteeing that tenant isolation is maintained at the database kernel level.

6.4 Formulas and Scoring Units

To provide an objective, data-backed foundation for early-stage evaluation, CampusVenture implements an algorithmic Investment Readiness Score (IRS) and a blended pre-money valuation engine:

1. Composite Investment Readiness Score ($IRS in [0, 100]$):
$$IRS = 0.25 cdot T + 0.20 cdot P + 0.15 cdot M + 0.15 cdot U + 0.15 cdot F + 0.10 cdot G$$
Where: $T$ = Team Completeness & Technical Execution (0–100), $P$ = Product IP & Prototype Stage (0–100), $M$ = Market Opportunity & Problem Scale (0–100), $U$ = University Incubation Cell Endorsement (0–100), $F$ = Financial Model & Unit Economics (0–100), and $G$ = Governance & NDA Compliance (0–100).
2. Diligence Risk Index ($DRI$):
$$DRI = 100 - IRS$$
A venture with $IRS ge 80$ is categorized as Institutional Diligence Ready ($DRI le 20$), permitting accelerated angel access.
3. Algorithmic Pre-Money Valuation ($Val_{pre}$):
$$Val_{pre} = Base_{regional} imes left( 1 + sum_{i=1}^{5} w_i cdot rac{S_i - 50}{50} ight)$$
Blends the Berkus Method and Scorecard Valuation Model with local collegiate angel benchmarks ($Base_{regional} = ext{INR } 1.50 ext{ Crores}$).
